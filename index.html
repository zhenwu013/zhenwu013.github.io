About Me

My research integrates geospatial analysis, remote sensing, and data-driven modeling to understand environmental change and its interactions with human systems.

My expertise includes modeling and analyzing urban disasters and climate gentrification under climate change, applying machine learning and big-data methods to carbon sink modeling, and conducting spatiotemporal analyses of land use and land cover change.

I also develop comprehensive indicator systems and quantitative models to assess coastal environmental health, with strong skills in mapping, geospatial visualization, and multi-source data integration.

Climate Risk Sea-level rise, urban flooding, extreme heat, and coastal vulnerability.
Carbon & Ecology Carbon sink estimation, ecosystem restoration, and land use change.
Spatial Methods GIS, remote sensing, GeoAI, and spatiotemporal modeling workflows.
